OUR PHILOSOPHY

A better way to live at home.

A home can already be beautiful and still have more to give.

A terrace can work harder. A garden can connect more naturally to the house. A kitchen can open into the way you entertain. An entire villa can be rethought around the way you live now.

DOMAIRE looks at the bigger picture. We bring design thinking, specialist expertise, construction and project coordination together so the finished result feels considered as a whole.

OUTDOOR LIVING

Sometimes the best room in the house isn't in the house.

On the Costa del Sol, outside is part of everyday life. It is where mornings start, where friends stay for dinner and where much of the year can be spent.

We consider the relationship between house and garden, sunlight and shade, cooking and dining, planting and materials, water, lighting and the way people naturally move through a space.

Make more of life outside.
